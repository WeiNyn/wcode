APrompt + cursor

The two glyphs the TUI is made of: the ❯ prompt and the ▌ live cursor. Today's >_ with the underscore promoted to a cursor block.

Buys Nothing to relearn: it still reads >_ at a glance.

Costs Category-typical — every CLI has a chevron.

BThe wordmark's w

The initial, drawn as four strokes on the same chevron family as ❯, with every acute vertex cut flat where a miter would draw a needle.

Buys The identity is the name, not a borrowed terminal trope.

Costs A letter says nothing about what the product does; dense at 16 px.

The live cursor made solid, with the prompt knocked out of it: one mass, one ink, and the only candidate whose mark and app tile are one shape.

Buys Reads as an app icon rather than a toolbar glyph.
